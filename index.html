Case study

175 conversions in one month from a $198 ad budget

Kimmie's Nail Spa is a local salon in Cheshire, Connecticut. I handled their full digital setup as an independent contractor (Nov 2025 – Nov 2026): website, Google Business Profile and Google Ads.

The challenge

A small local business needed more bookings from people searching nearby, on a modest ad budget, with no in-house marketing help.

What I did
  1. Built the website (kimmiesnailspa.com) with GoDaddy Website Builder and Airo, including domain, DNS and email setup.
  2. Launched Performance Max first to build brand awareness in the local area.
  3. Shifted the main budget to a Search lead-generation campaign, keeping PMax on a low maintenance budget.
  4. Optimized every week: keywords, search terms, budgets, conversions and optimization score.
  5. Improved local visibility by optimizing the Google Business Profile and creating all ad and website creatives in Canva.
  6. Reported results and recommendations directly to the owner in plain language.
